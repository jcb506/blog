# About me

I am a cybersecurity engineer with a varied background ranging from Linux administration to data science.

I have a strong interest in understanding how things work, investigating technical problems, and developing solutions.

In this blog, I plan to share my experiments and projects related to cybersecurity, Linux, and a bit of SRE.

My work involves using data analysis to develop detection rules for an anti-bot solution. Combined with my interest in malware analysis, this means the blog will have a strong focus on bot detection, threat analysis, and malware research.
